# Questions for the specification

Points where the examples or the specification (`presk-specs`) left the
compiler a choice, numbered in the order they were asked, each with the
provisional answer it implemented. The language reference
(`presk-specs/reference/presk-reference.tex`) is the normative statement of
the language, and its chapter 8, "Open questions", is the list of record:
its table `tab:open-compiler` states each question's state (closed, closed
otherwise than the compiler, restated, open with the W-item that settles
it, or outside the language) and the definition, requirement or section
that grounds it. The status column below is that table's, its numbers the
reference's at draft v0.12 (marked at plan 0.10.0 step 8); a question
asked after it is marked from the compiler's decision until the reference
lists it. The files `presk-specs/implementation/presk-spec-questions-0.X.0.md`
archive the questions by version.
